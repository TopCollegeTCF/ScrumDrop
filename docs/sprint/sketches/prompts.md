# Эскизы ImageGen

Эскизы показывают оформление. Для спринта цена кейса — 30 монет, стартовый баланс — 100.

## Каталог и результат

UI mockup landscape for ScrumDrop: Russian catalog and case opening result with a typography-made toy chest and crystal. Normal highly readable contemporary sans-serif Russian headings and buttons (never ASCII lettering). Black background, subtle dark violet panels (#0C0032,#190061,#240090,#3500D3). Stylish editorial cover aesthetic, large clean margins. The central artwork is a sweeping luminous S-shaped violet aurora ribbon with one narrow grassy lime-green core, made of visibly distinct monospaced punctuation characters of varying density. All illustrative decoration is character art. No hacker/terminal aesthetic. Violet dominates; green sparingly. Sharp typography.
